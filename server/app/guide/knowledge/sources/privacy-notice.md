# ZoikoStream Privacy Policy

## About this policy

The privacy policy (/privacy) is titled "What we collect, and what we do with it." It describes the ZoikoStream platform and the ZoikoStream Android app. The app adds no collection of its own: it runs the same client, talks to the same API, and stores nothing on the device beyond the session the policy describes. Where a detail is governed by a lifecycle (the processor list, the notice text itself), the policy links to the thing that governs it.

## What ZoikoStream collects

When you create an account, ZoikoStream stores your name, email address and password. The password is hashed and is never readable by ZoikoStream or by anyone with database access. If you join an organization, ZoikoStream records your role in it.

When you attend or host an event, ZoikoStream processes the live audio and video of that event to deliver it to its audience. Live media is carried by ZoikoStream's streaming provider for the duration of the event; it is not stored by the app on your device. Recordings exist only where an event's organizer explicitly creates one, under the retention rules that organizer configures.

When you contact support or report a security issue, ZoikoStream keeps the message you sent so it can respond.

## How ZoikoStream uses it

Your account data is used to sign you in, to deliver the events you are invited to, and to send you operational email about those events: invitations, assignment notifications, verification links and security notices. These are not marketing messages, and unsubscribing from marketing mail does not stop them.

ZoikoStream keeps audit and security logs of significant actions (sign-ins, event operations, administrative changes) to detect and investigate abuse. These logs are not public and are not sold or shared for advertising. ZoikoStream runs no advertising of any kind.

## Who processes it

ZoikoStream uses a small number of providers to operate the service: transactional email, live streaming and media ingest, payment processing, object storage and managed Redis. The authoritative, versioned list of processors is published in the Privacy Center (/organization/privacy) and is updated before a change takes effect where it requires notice.

## Your rights

You can request a copy of your personal data, ask ZoikoStream to correct it, restrict its processing, object to it, or ask ZoikoStream to delete it. Requests are made through the Privacy Center (/organization/privacy). Every request gets a reference you can quote, and you can check its status there at any time.

For export and deletion, ZoikoStream verifies that the request really comes from you, by confirming control of the email address, before acting. That verification is required even when you are signed in.

Where records must be retained to meet a legal obligation (for example billing records, or a recording under legal hold), the deletion completion message tells you exactly which categories of record survived and why. ZoikoStream does not claim total erasure when residue lawfully remains.

## How ZoikoStream protects it

Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS only; the app refuses cleartext connections). Access tokens live in your browser's local storage and are never backed up off the device by the Android app. Data exports are delivered as single-use links that expire, not as email attachments. If you believe you have found a security issue, report it through the Trust Center (/trust).

## Children

ZoikoStream is a business product and is not directed at children. ZoikoStream does not knowingly create accounts for anyone under the age required by their jurisdiction for consent to data processing, and will delete an account it learns was created by a child.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes are published through the notice lifecycle in the Privacy Center (/organization/privacy), which keeps every published version readable, including superseded ones, and tells you when a change needs your consent.

## Contacting ZoikoStream about privacy

- Privacy requests: through the Privacy Center (/organization/privacy), so they are logged with a reference. The privacy policy page also links there as "Manage your data".
- Security matters: through the Trust Center (/trust).
- Everything else: the contact page (/contact).
