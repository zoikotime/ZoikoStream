# ZoikoStream Pricing and Plans

## Published pricing

ZoikoStream's public website does not publish plan prices. No plan names, prices, currencies or billing frequencies appear on the public pages.

## How to get pricing or commercial information

1. Go to the contact page (/contact). The homepage "Talk to an expert" button also opens it.
2. Choose the topic "Procurement, licensing & commercial terms" for pricing, licensing and commercial questions, or "Platform evaluation & enterprise sales" to evaluate the platform.
3. Describe what you need and select "Send inquiry". The ZoikoStream team will get back to you.

## Getting started

To start planning an event, select "Plan your live event" on the homepage, which goes to sign-up (/signup).
