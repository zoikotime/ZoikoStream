# ZoikoStream Product Overview

## What ZoikoStream is

ZoikoStream offers live events. Its homepage headline is: "Bring every important moment to everyone who matters."

ZoikoStream provides professionally managed live events with secure audience access, captions, translation, recording and replay, built for occasions that need to work the first time.

## Key highlights

The ZoikoStream homepage highlights four things:

- Secure delivery
- Accessible experiences
- Recording & replay
- Expert support

## Live event readiness areas

The homepage shows an illustrative "Live Event Readiness" card. It is labeled "Illustrative" and does not show the status of any real event. The readiness areas it lists are:

- Source
- Audience access
- Captions
- Recording & replay
- Event support

## Why organizations choose ZoikoStream

The homepage lists these reasons:

- Enterprise-grade security
- Global infrastructure
- Privacy by design
- Human support

The homepage also states: "Trusted by organizations worldwide."

## How to get started

The homepage offers two next steps:

1. Select "Plan your live event" to go to sign-up (/signup).
2. Select "Talk to an expert" to go to the contact page (/contact), where ZoikoStream routes your inquiry to the team best equipped to help.
