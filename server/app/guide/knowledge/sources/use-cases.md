# Occasions ZoikoStream Covers

## Event types

The ZoikoStream homepage lists these occasions:

- Memorials: Honor and remember with dignity.
- Worship Services: Bring your community together online.
- Weddings & Celebrations: Celebrate love with those you invite.
- Graduations: Milestones shared worldwide.
- Civic Events: Connect and inform your community.
- Conferences: Engage audiences anywhere.
- Corporate Events: Communicate and connect globally.

## Planning an event for one of these occasions

1. Select "Plan your live event" on the homepage to go to sign-up (/signup).
2. Or, to talk to the team first, go to the contact page (/contact) and choose the topic "Live Events & managed broadcasting". The contact page says: "Start your event brief and our team will guide you through every step."
