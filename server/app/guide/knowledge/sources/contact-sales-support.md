# Contacting ZoikoStream Sales and Support

## How to reach ZoikoStream

The contact page (/contact) is titled "Talk to the right expert, faster." It asks you to tell ZoikoStream what you're trying to accomplish, and ZoikoStream will route your inquiry to the team best equipped to help. The "Talk to an expert" button on the homepage opens this page.

## Contact topics

The "What can we help with?" field offers these topics:

- Platform evaluation & enterprise sales
- API, SDK & integrations
- Live Events & managed broadcasting
- Security, privacy & compliance
- Procurement, licensing & commercial terms
- Technical architecture & implementation
- Partnerships & strategic opportunities
- Existing customer support
- Other

## How to send an inquiry

1. Go to /contact.
2. Enter your first name, last name and work email (all required).
3. Optionally enter your organization name.
4. Select your country or region (required).
5. Choose a topic under "What can we help with?" (required).
6. Under "How can we help?", describe your goals, environment, or the details of your inquiry (required, up to 1,500 characters).
7. Select "Send inquiry".

When the message is sent, the page shows: "Thanks! Your message has been sent. Our team will get back to you shortly." If sending fails, your details stay in the form so you can try again.

By submitting the form, you acknowledge the ZoikoStream Privacy Notice.

## What not to include

For your security, do not include passwords, API keys, credentials, or other sensitive production data in a contact inquiry.

## Shortcuts on the contact page

The "Looking for something specific?" panel preselects a topic for you:

- Planning a Live Event? Start your event brief and the team will guide you through every step. (Topic: Live Events & managed broadcasting.)
- Need technical documentation? Ask for developer docs, API references, SDKs and integration guides. (Topic: API, SDK & integrations.)
- Already using ZoikoStream? Get account support, report an issue, or check status. (Topic: Existing customer support.)

## Existing customers

If you already have an account, select "Sign in" on the contact page to go to /login.

## Other channels for specific requests

- Security issues: report them through the Trust Center (/trust).
- Privacy requests: make them through the Privacy Center (/organization/privacy), so they are logged with a reference.
- Service status: see the status page (/status).
