# Viewer Help: Captions and Replay

## Turning captions on or off

When a stream carries captions, the player shows a "CC" button.

1. Select "CC" ("Turn captions on") to show captions over the video.
2. Select "CC" again ("Turn captions off") to hide them.

## After an event ends

When an event ends, the player shows "This event has ended" and one of these replay messages:

- "The replay is available." Select "Watch the replay" to play it. If the replay has an end date, the player shows "Available until" followed by that date.
- "The replay is being prepared. This page will update automatically."
- "The replay is no longer available."
- "No recording is available for this event."

## Watching a replay

While watching a replay, you can seek through the recording and use the same player settings as for a live stream, including playback speed and Picture-in-Picture.

## Downloading an event

The event page does not offer a download of the live stream or the replay. When the organizer makes a replay available, you watch it on the event page with "Watch the replay".

## Who decides whether there is a recording

Recordings exist only where an event's organizer explicitly creates one, under the retention rules that organizer configures.
