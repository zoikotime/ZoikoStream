## Peak viewers
The highest number of people watching one event at the same moment. "Peak viewers (summed)" adds each event's peak together; it is not a count of unique viewers.

## Watch time
Total time viewers spent watching, measured by the 15-second sampler. Events that were not measured are excluded rather than counted as zero.

## Engagement
An engagement score per event, averaged across events that had an audience. It is not shown when there was no audience to measure.

## Unique attendees and average watch time
Estimated values: people who joined at least one event, and the average minutes watched per attendee.

## Show rate
The share of invited people who attended. It is calculated only for private, invite-only events.

## Sessions and rejoins
Viewing sessions admitted to the live stream, and the share of viewers who left and joined again.

## Audience insights
Geography (from viewer country where known), device mix, browsers, operating systems, registration to attendance, and refused join attempts by reason. Device, location and traffic-source breakdowns in the main analytics view need metering that is not yet available.

## Good experience
"Good experience" is not a metric. Guide shows the measurable parts instead: playback start success, failed sessions, rejoins and watch time.
