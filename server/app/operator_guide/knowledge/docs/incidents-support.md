## Event incidents
An event incident records a problem affecting one event: severity, cause, impact, a customer summary and the next update time. Its operational state is delayed, temporary hold, resumed or canceled.

## Platform status
The public status page lists published incidents (investigating, identified, monitoring, resolved) with their impact, and scheduled or emergency maintenance. Guide never declares a platform outage from one event's symptoms.

## How Guide labels signals
- Confirmed — an incident record exists.
- Detected — a platform monitor reports it.
- Suspected — Guide's interpretation of indirect evidence; always disclosed as such.
- Unknown — no current data.

## Support cases
A support case is open, in progress, waiting for customer, resolved, closed or reopened, with a priority and a next update time. Waiting for customer means the support team needs something from you.

## Support access
ZoikoStream staff can access your organization's data only through a support access request that an organization admin approves, for a limited time and limited actions. Guide never grants cross-organization access.
